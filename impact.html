Research Impact & Translation

Evidence of peer-reviewed research being discussed, cited or translated into public, professional, patient and practitioner-facing resources beyond academic citation, including mainstream media, professional bodies, clinical evidence databases and patient advocacy organisations.

The Paradoxical Effect of Creatine Monohydrate on Muscle Damage Markers: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

Sports Medicine, 2022  ·  Co-author

  • Washington PostMainstream media

    Referenced the meta-analysis when explaining that creatine may reduce some markers of exercise-induced muscle damage after acute strenuous exercise, while longer-term supplementation may interact differently with training stress.

  • Operation Supplement SafetyPublic health / military guidance

    Cited the review in public guidance on creatine monohydrate for athletes and Service Members, noting that creatine may reduce immediate post-exercise muscle damage while more research is needed on dose and timing.

  • Transparent LabsCommercial fitness content

    Used the paper's acute-versus-chronic training distinction in its "Muscle Damage + Inflammation" section, discussing whether creatine improves recovery.

Effects of Plyometric Jump Training on Balance Performance in Healthy Participants: A Systematic Review With Meta-Analysis

Frontiers in Physiology, 2021  ·  First author

  • Yoga Medicine — "Balance Myths Busted"Practitioner education / podcast

    Listed the paper as part of the research base for a practitioner-facing discussion of balance as a trainable quality, including the role of plyometric jump training in improving static and dynamic balance performance.

Changes in Lower Limb Biomechanics Across Various Stages of Maturation and Implications for ACL Injury Risk in Female Athletes: a Systematic Review

Sports Medicine, 2024  ·  First and corresponding author

  • UK Strength and Conditioning AssociationProfessional body

    Cited the review to support coaching recommendations that practitioners should consider biological age, maturity status and neuromuscular development, rather than chronological age alone, when assessing ACL-relevant biomechanical risk in post-pubertal girls.

Influence of Neuromuscular Training Interventions on Jump-Landing Biomechanics and Implications for ACL Injuries in Youth Females: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis

Sports Medicine, 2025  ·  First author

  • PEDro / Physiotherapy ChoicesConsumer-facing clinical summary

    Summarised the review for physiotherapy and clinical audiences, highlighting improvements in ACL-relevant landing mechanics such as increased peak knee flexion angle and reduced knee valgus motion in youth females.

Post-polio hospital admissions in Australia over a 10-year period: An observational study and analysis of trends by month, location, and comparable conditions

  • Polio Australia / Polio Health research databasePatient / advocacy resource

    Listed and summarised the study for post-polio stakeholders, presenting it as work that improves estimates of post-polio conditions in Australia and where affected people are hospitalised.

  • Polio Australia Annual ReportAdvocacy reporting

  • Polio Place / Post-Polio Health InternationalPatient / advocacy resource

    Included the paper in its public medical-articles database, making the hospitalisation findings discoverable to post-polio patient, community and advocacy audiences.

Effects of Muscle Strengthening and Cardiovascular Fitness Activities for Poliomyelitis Survivors: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis

Journal of Rehabilitation Medicine, 2021  ·  First author

  • PEDroClinical evidence database
